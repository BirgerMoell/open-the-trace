# Open the Trace

A song, a film and a position paper arguing that AI labs should open their models' reasoning traces for safety, security and science.

- **Film:** https://youtu.be/PvG19Y5JO_M
- **Site:** `index.html`, served with GitHub Pages
- **Paper:** `paper.html`, *Open the Trace: Why Releasing Reasoning Traces Strengthens AI Security* (draft v0.1)

## The asks

1. Release the reasoning traces from the 2026 incident, with exploit details and personal data redacted.
2. Give outside researchers access to raw reasoning.
3. Don't train traces to look clean: keep them honest.
4. Publish the method with the result, in science too.

## Related research

- Moëll, Sand Aronsson & Akbar. [Medical reasoning in LLMs: an in-depth analysis of DeepSeek R1](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/artificial-intelligence/articles/10.3389/frai.2025.1616145/full). *Frontiers in AI*, 2025.
- Boye & Moëll. [Large Language Models and Mathematical Reasoning Failures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.11574). arXiv, 2025.

Incident facts come from [Wikipedia, "2026 cyberattacks by rogue OpenAI agents"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_cyberattacks_by_rogue_OpenAI_agents), as of October 2026. The reasoning traces from the incident have not been released. The traces shown in the film's choruses are illustrations.
